Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about courses, membership, payments, certificates, and support at EDCEPTA EDUCATION.

What is EDCEPTA EDUCATION?

EDCEPTA EDUCATION is an online learning platform offering career-focused programs in technology and professional skills—including AI & ML, web development, data science, cloud & DevOps, certification prep, and more. Learn more on our About Us page.

How do I enroll in a course?

Browse programs on the Courses page, select a course, and add it to your cart or choose Buy Now. Create a free account or sign in at Login, then complete checkout. Once enrolled, access your courses from your user dashboard.

What is EDCEPTA Plus membership?

EDCEPTA Plus is our annual membership plan (₹200/year) that includes member-only resources, priority support, live Q&A sessions with educators, and a digital certificate when you complete your plan. See details on the Membership section of our homepage.

Do I get a certificate after completing a course?

Yes. Eligible programs include a certificate of completion from EDCEPTA EDUCATION when you finish the required lessons and assessments. Certification exam vouchers (AWS, CompTIA, PMI, etc.) are separate and subject to availability.

How long do I have access to a course?

Most purchased courses include lifetime access to materials on our platform, so you can revisit lessons and resources at your own pace. Specific terms may vary for bundles or promotions and will be shown at checkout.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on eligible course purchases if you have consumed less than 20% of the content. Full details are in our Return Policy. Email educepta@gmail.com to request a refund.

Are payments secure? What if payment is unavailable?

We use secure payment processing for purchases. If online payment is temporarily unavailable on the site, you may see a notice at checkout—please contact us and our team will help you complete enrollment or answer billing questions.

Can I access courses on mobile?

Yes. EDCEPTA EDUCATION works in modern web browsers on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices so you can learn wherever you are.

How do I reset my password or get login help?

Go to the Login page and use the sign-in form with your registered email. If you cannot access your account, email educepta@gmail.com with your registered email address and we will assist you.

How can I contact support?

Reach us via the Contact Us page, email educepta@gmail.com, or call +91 8287693368. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
