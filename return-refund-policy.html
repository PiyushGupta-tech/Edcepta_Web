Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We strive to process requests as quickly as possible. Please review our guidelines below:

1. Cancellation Window

To be eligible for a full refund, cancellation requests must be sent to our support email address within 4 hours of the initial purchase.

Send your request to support@educepta.in with the subject line Refund Request. Include your registered email, course or membership name, and payment reference.

2. Processing Your Refund

Once your request is reviewed and approved:

Method: The refund will be credited automatically to your original method of payment within 7 business days.

3. Confirmation

You will receive an automated email confirmation from our system as soon as the refund has been successfully issued.

4. Contact Us

For refund or cancellation questions, visit Contact Us or email support@educepta.in.
